The Gap

The World Is Changing and Classrooms Look the Same.

Schools have technology for almost everything now, except the loop that actually drives learning: assign, grade, understand, adjust. That gap costs everyone something different. Teachers lose evenings to grading by hand. Students wait days for feedback that would only help if it came back in minutes. Administrators run a school on spreadsheets stitched together from a dozen teachers' best guesses.

Teachers: grading eats evenings Tutors: no easy way to track 1:1 progress Students: feedback arrives too late to use Admins: no real-time view of the school
The Solution

One AI Engine, Reused Differently by Everyone Running a Classroom.

InnedAI reads handwriting across every question type, scores free-response answers against a real rubric with partial credit, and turns the results into something useful for whoever's looking: a finished grade for a teacher, same-day feedback for a student, a live picture of the whole school for an admin.

The Platform

Built for Everyone Around a Classroom, Not Just the Person Grading It.

Four views of the same engine: teachers get their evenings back, tutors get a classroom's worth of tools without the classroom, students get feedback that actually helps, and admins get visibility they don't have to ask for.
