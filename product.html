The Product

Everything InnedAI Does, Screen by Screen

From the first photo of a paper test to a released grade in a student's hand: every step of the pipeline, and what it looks like along the way.

01 · Grading

Photograph a Stack. Get It Graded.

Bulk-scan a class set of paper answer sheets with automatic student identification. InnedAI grades multiple choice, short answer, and full free-response essays in one pass, scoring against a rubric with partial credit and flagging anything worth a second look.

02 · Digitizing

Already Have a Paper Test? Just Photograph It.

Upload or photograph an existing exam and InnedAI extracts every question, infers point values and difficulty, and writes grading rubrics for the essay questions automatically, so it's gradable the moment it's digitized.

03 · Content Creation

Build a New Assessment in Seconds.

Generate a full test from a textbook unit, a topic, or a quick prompt, complete with distractors, explanations, and point values. Shuffle it into A/B/C variants to prevent copying, or simplify it for an IEP reading level, without changing what's being assessed.

A Full Lesson Plan, on Demand.

From just a subject, grade, and topic, get a complete, timed lesson plan: objectives, activities, a formative-assessment strategy, differentiation notes, and standards alignment. Chat with it to adjust length, difficulty, or focus.

Reach Every Student, Not Just Some.

Simplified reading-level variants for IEP students, and full test translation for English language learners. One tap, same underlying rubric, so grading stays consistent no matter which version a student takes.
